Support for Students & Institutions

A trusted technology partner for college students, engineering departments, educational institutions, and project developers.

Academic Partnership

Moving from Theory to Practical Prototyping

HV Vertex Electronics aims to become a useful technology partner for college students, engineering students, educational institutions, and project developers. We believe innovation begins in student labs and maker workshops, and our mission is to make real engineering components, microcontrollers, and debugging tools accessible to every aspiring builder.

Offerings

What We Provide

Comprehensive hardware, toolchains, and technical assistance for academic success.

Development Boards

Arduino, ESP32, and HV Vertex own-branded development boards tailored for experimentation, coding, and fast hardware flashing.

Electronic Components

Active and passive components, relays, power regulators, optocouplers, connectors, and breadboard-friendly discrete electronics.

Sensors & Modules

Temperature, ultrasonic, infrared, gas, PIR motion, accelerometer, and environmental sensors for diverse monitoring experiments.

Robotics Components

Chassis kits, wheel assemblies, DC & BLDC motor drivers, servo controllers, fasteners, and brackets for multi-terrain robotic builds.

Project Hardware

Turnkey hardware bundles, power supplies, custom wiring harnesses, and fasteners essential for academic mini and major capstones.

Embedded & IoT Solutions

Assistance in architecting Wi-Fi/Bluetooth mesh networks, sensor-to-cloud pipelines, and microcontroller firmware integration.

Prototype Development

Rapid proof-of-concept testing, PCB circuit layout guidance, and modular hardware assembly support to bring ideas to reality.

Practical Learning Resources

Curated wiring guides, pinout diagrams, sample algorithms, and intuitive toolchain support through our programming app.

Academic Project Assistance

Mentorship and hardware troubleshooting for student teams building final-year engineering projects and hackathon submissions.

"The objective is to encourage students to move from theoretical learning toward practical experimentation, prototyping, innovation, and real-world problem solving."

— HV Vertex Educational Mission
