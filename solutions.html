Smart Automation Solutions

Turning ordinary products and everyday processes into smarter, safer, more efficient, and connected systems.

Our Philosophy

Problem-to-Solution Engineering

Instead of developing technology for its own sake, HV Vertex focuses on identifying real-world friction in everyday living, workplaces, and workshops — creating targeted, accessible automated answers.

1. Intelligent Home Automation

Complete control systems designed for households and modern living spaces:

  • Lighting Control: Automated ambient and scheduled illumination tailored to room occupancy.
  • Fan & Climate Control: Smart automatic temperature-based fan controllers and climate triggers.
  • Air Conditioning Integration: AC control modules for optimized comfort and power conservation.
  • Appliance Management: Centralized and automated operation of high-consumption domestic appliances.

2. Energy Efficiency & Smart Scheduling

Intelligent systems that eliminate wasteful power usage throughout the day:

  • Sensor-Triggered Lighting: Automatic turn-off when rooms and hallways are unoccupied.
  • Intelligent Duty Cycles: Programmable time-schedules that match usage patterns.
  • Thermal-Responsive Power: Controllers regulating speeds and power draws based on environmental sensors.
  • Measurable Reduction: Designed to significantly cut electricity bills without compromising comfort.

3. Remote Control via Wi-Fi & Bluetooth

Wireless micro-controllers and IoT modules empowering seamless remote management:

  • Wireless Connectivity: Embedded ESP32 and Bluetooth/Wi-Fi wireless communication boards.
  • Smart Phone Interface: Easy integration with mobile dashboards and connected touchpoints.
  • Local & Cloud Flexibility: Operate devices seamlessly within your local network or remotely.
  • Status Telemetry: Real-time feedback and state reporting for all connected switches.

4. Retrofit Automation Modules

Upgrade existing appliances into smart machines without costly total replacements:

  • Non-Destructive Upgrade: Compact form-factor retrofit modules that fit behind conventional switchboards.
  • Preserve Existing Infrastructure: Transform legacy fans, lights, and appliances into smart systems.
  • Dual Operation: Retains manual physical toggle switch control alongside automated overrides.
  • Cost-Effective Smart Transition: Ideal for residential renovations, offices, and educational institutions.

1
